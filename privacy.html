Robot: privacy policy

Robot is a personal assistant used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events. Access is read-only: it cannot send, change or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is read on the owner's own computer to answer the owner's questions and to prepare spoken summaries. To produce an answer, the relevant text is sent to the AI model service the owner uses. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Storage

Robot does not keep a copy of the mailbox or calendar. A short log of the owner's own conversations with the assistant is kept on the owner's computer.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account settings, under third-party access.
